# Source Audit Update — 2026-11-19

## Scope

Read-only verification of the Android 15 / kernel 4.19 source pairing for Redmi Note 5A Lite (`ugglite`) in the `ahmadsubqi/OneUI7-Ugglite` preparation project.

No Android build, device modification, flashing, or repartitioning was performed.

## Confirmed source references

### Android 15 device tree

Repository: `Mi-Thorium/android_device_xiaomi_Mi8937`
Ref: `a15_qpr2/master`

The ref is resolvable through GitHub. Inspected files:

- `BoardConfig.mk` — blob `b1d6717200ff1f79b0b58432957d1e216f1c756c`
- `device.mk` — blob `9b41f24376c2f76484460a101b9defd88682c48c`
- `lineage_Mi8937_4_19.mk` — blob `552c7e39c6e49b3ea56fab6e5af8ffa6fdc4e703`
- `AndroidProducts.mk` — blob `01941898673f97c0d126f302c707d0d599468429`
- `lineage.dependencies` — blob `111ae3279e2a8029499d4f689d40ea3551bbcbf2`

Findings:

- The product target is `lineage_Mi8937_4_19-userdebug`, with product device `Mi8937_4_19`; there is no separate ugglite lunch target.
- `BoardConfig.mk` includes `ugglite` in `TARGET_OTA_ASSERT_DEVICE`.
- `device.mk` explicitly packages `xiaomi_ugglite_overlay`.
- The 4.19 product inherits the unified Mi8937 device configuration.
- `TARGET_SUPPORT_HAL1 := false` for kernel 4.19. Legacy Camera HAL1 compatibility must not be assumed.
- The device tree declares retrofit dynamic partitions and partition sizes/layout assumptions. These are not confirmed against the physical handset.

### Kernel manifest and source

Repository: `Mi-Thorium/kernel_manifest`
Ref: `mithorium-4.19`
File: `default.xml`
Blob: `a5d339482c0d422e36e4929e8a3d6bb68446b81d`

The manifest maps the following projects:

- `kernel_msm-4.19` at `mithorium/a13/master`, manifest path `kernel`
- `kernel_devicetree_msm-4.19` at `dts/master`
- `kernel_devicetree_xiaomi-msm8937` at `dts/mi8937/4.19/master`
- `kernel_techpack_xiaomi-msm8937` at `techpack/mi8937/4.19/master`
- Additional audio, camera and WLAN projects are also declared.

Kernel checks:

- `kernel_msm-4.19/Makefile` reports Linux `4.19.325` (file blob `c9686d18bd53c36702c3183967c421744e2f7cf0`).
- `arch/arm64/configs/vendor/msm8937-perf_defconfig` exists (blob `9fd02c1e6bb2e8af094ab955d32b2d12aa2d1ed0`) and enables `CONFIG_ARCH_MSM8937=y`.
- Xiaomi MSM8937 DTS Makefile includes `ugglite.dtb` (blob `26a7f9a9ebb219e159c98b7e517ea93924327a86`).
- `ugglite.dts` exists (blob `fabcb53a14df7ae50ecf088a4e203b076756f04e`) and identifies `xiaomi,ugglite` as a compatible device.

## Important distinction: blob SHAs vs commit SHAs

The hashes recorded above are Git blob IDs for inspected files. They are **not** repository commit IDs and must not be used as pinned manifest revisions.

## Remaining blockers

1. Resolve and record immutable commit SHAs for every Android device, common, vendor, kernel, DTS and techpack project.
2. Confirm how the separate kernel manifest is intended to be integrated with the Android source tree. Its `path="kernel"` checkout must be reconciled with the device common tree's expected `kernel/xiaomi/mithorium-4.19/kernel` source path. Do not assume a nested `repo init` is correct.
3. Inspect ugglite DTS include dependencies and verify the matching DTS subproject revisions.
4. Verify vendor blob provenance and compatibility; the presence of old proprietary blobs is not proof of Android 15 compatibility.
5. Only after source revisions and paths are reproducibly pinned, attempt a non-flashing Android 15 baseline build.

## Current assessment

**Source family pairing is credible and ugglite-specific support is present in the inspected Android 15 device tree and 4.19 DTS. The source set is not yet reproducibly locked or build-ready.**

No flashable One UI 7 ROM exists. Do not flash, repartition, relock the bootloader, or downgrade firmware based on this audit. The physical handset is still unavailable for live ADB and partition verification.
